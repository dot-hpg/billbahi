GST invoice number rules, with examples you can copy

GST does not prescribe one numbering format, but it sets a few rules your series must follow.

The rules

  • At most 16 characters.
  • Only letters, numbers, hyphen (-) and slash (/).
  • Consecutive, and unique for each financial year (April to March).
  • You can run separate series, for example one per branch or one for exports.
