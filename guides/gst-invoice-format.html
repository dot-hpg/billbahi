GST invoice format: the fields every tax invoice must have

If you are registered under GST, each tax invoice you issue has to carry a set of details listed in Rule 46 of the CGST Rules. Missing one can cost your customer their input tax credit, so it is worth getting right.

Mandatory fields

FieldWhat to write
Supplier detailsYour name, address and GSTIN
Invoice numberA consecutive serial number, up to 16 characters, unique for the financial year. Letters, numbers, hyphen and slash are allowed.
Invoice dateDate of issue
Recipient detailsName, address and GSTIN if the buyer is registered
Unregistered buyerFor invoices of ₹50,000 or more, the buyer's name and address and the delivery address with state name and code
HSN or SAC code4 digits if your turnover was up to ₹5 crore, 6 digits above that
Description, quantity and unitWhat you supplied
Total and taxable valueValue of supply and the taxable value after any discount
Tax rate and amountCGST and SGST/UTGST for supplies within a state, IGST for supplies between states
Place of supplyState name and code, required for inter-state supplies
Reverse chargeWhether tax is payable on reverse charge
SignatureSignature or digital signature of the supplier or authorised person

Common mistakes

  • Restarting invoice numbers in the middle of a financial year, or reusing a number.
  • Charging CGST + SGST on a sale to another state. That should be IGST.
  • Leaving out the place of supply on inter-state invoices.
  • Wrong HSN/SAC digits for your turnover.
