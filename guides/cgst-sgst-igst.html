CGST + SGST or IGST? How to pick the right tax on your invoice

The rate stays the same. What changes is how the tax is split, and that depends on two states: where you are registered, and the place of supply.

The rule

  • Same state (intra-state): charge CGST and SGST, each half of the rate. 18% becomes 9% CGST + 9% SGST.
  • Same Union Territory without a legislature (Chandigarh, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu): CGST + UTGST instead of SGST.
  • Different states (inter-state): charge the full rate as IGST. 18% becomes 18% IGST.

What is the place of supply?

For goods it is usually where the goods are delivered. For most services to a registered business it is the location of that business. The first two digits of a GSTIN are the state code, so a buyer GSTIN starting with 29 is in Karnataka. Special rules apply to some services such as events, transport and real estate.
